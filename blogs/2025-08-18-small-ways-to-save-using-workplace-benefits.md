What do you think of when you think of saving money? Is it making homemade coffee and skipping Starbucks, getting into meal prepping, cutting coupons, or only eating out during happy hour? These are all great ways to save a few bucks, but one of the most overlooked ways to stretch your budget is right where you spend most of your day—at work.
